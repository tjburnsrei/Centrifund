# Jim's calling guide

1. Open the calling link in Safari and enter the shared password. The app remembers your sign-in.
2. Choose a contact from **Up next**. Due follow-ups appear first.
3. Review the brief. Tap **Save to iPhone** if you want their details in Contacts, then confirm the iPhone contact card.
4. Tap **Call**. Your iPhone calls using your normal number. Return to Safari afterward.
5. Choose an outcome. For a conversation, tap **Record notes**, speak, then **Stop recording**. You can type instead.
6. Tap **Organize my notes**. Review the note, next action, and date; correct anything that needs changing.
7. Tap **Save & next**. Wait for **Call saved to CRM**.

**No answer?** Choose No answer and Save & next.

**Wrong number?** Choose the number you tried, then Bad number. Other numbers stay available.

**Do not call again?** Choose Do not call. The contact leaves the calling queue.

**Connection or AI problem?** Keep the app open if possible. Unfinished notes stay on this phone. Reconnect and retry, or use the transcript/type a note. “Saved on this phone” means it has not yet been saved to the CRM.

**One important recording tip:** Stay in Safari while dictating. An incoming call or locking your phone may interrupt recording.

**Already called someone?** Use All contacts to see their history.
