# Centrifund CRM — Jim's guide

## Put Centrifund CRM on your iPhone

1. Open [Centrifund CRM](https://centrifund-calling.vercel.app) in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share** (the square with an upward arrow), then **Add to Home Screen**. Scroll down in the Share menu if needed.
3. Turn on **Open as Web App** if shown, keep the name **Centrifund CRM**, and tap **Add**.
4. Tap the Centrifund CRM icon on your Home Screen. Enter the shared password if asked. Use this icon for your calls from now on.

Save unfinished notes to the CRM before switching from Safari to the Home Screen app. Notes saved only on your phone stay in the browser or app where you created them. Internet is required to open contacts and save to the CRM.

## Make a call

1. Open Centrifund CRM from your Home Screen (or the CRM link in Safari) and enter the shared password if asked. The app remembers your sign-in.
2. Choose a contact from **Up next**. Due follow-ups appear first.
3. Review the brief. Tap **Save to iPhone** if you want their details in Contacts, then confirm the iPhone contact card.
4. Tap **Call**. Your iPhone calls using your normal number. Return to Centrifund CRM afterward.
5. Choose an outcome. For a conversation, tap **Record notes**, speak, then **Stop recording**. You can type instead.
6. Tap **Organize my notes**. Review the note, next action, and date; correct anything that needs changing.
7. Tap **Save & next**. Wait for **Call saved to CRM**.

**No answer?** Choose No answer and Save & next.

**Wrong number?** Choose the number you tried, then Bad number. Other numbers stay available.

**Do not call again?** Choose Do not call. The contact leaves the calling queue.

**Connection or AI problem?** Keep the app open if possible. Unfinished notes stay on this phone. Reconnect and retry, or use the transcript/type a note. “Saved on this phone” means it has not yet been saved to the CRM.

**One important recording tip:** Keep Centrifund CRM open while dictating. An incoming call or locking your phone may interrupt recording.

**Already called someone?** Use All contacts to see their history.
